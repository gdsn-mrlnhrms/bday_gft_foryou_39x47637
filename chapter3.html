About a week ago, I told you "I'll tell you something later"

One day I was praying, I was thinking. Do I even love God? I was confused. Because I don't know what Love is in words. Sometimes I feel, but Idk if that's Love. Then I was reading the Bible and I saw a few verses about Love. Here it is: "Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs. Love does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth. It always protects, always trusts, always hopes, always perseveres. Love never fails."
After reading this, I thought about people whom I love like this, with exception of my family ofc. Suddenly you just came into my mind, I am not lying, God is my witness. This is the love I have for you Rem. I thought about it a lot and, It sometimes scares me that how can I love you this way yet I doubt if I love God. so I just erase the thoughts of all, but the truth stays same, this is why I always wait for you. Because no matter how long it takes, when you come back to me, it gives me happiness. So this is what I wanted to tell you then. I only realised this few weeks back. And now I know, that I truly love you not just the way the world see, but also how Bible says (even tho there is more which I can't put here beacuse it is so long). And in truth, It's not just empty words or emotions that will fade, but something beyond all these.
